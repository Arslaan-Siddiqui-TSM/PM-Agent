## YOUR TASK

Revise the feasibility report by:

1. **Addressing Critical Issues**: Fix all blocking concerns identified in the critique
2. **Implementing High Priority Improvements**: Make important enhancements
3. **Adding Missing Information**: Include all required details
4. **Improving Clarity**: Enhance sections that were unclear
5. **Preserving Strengths**: Keep positive aspects and well-written sections
6. **Maintaining Structure**: Keep the original report structure unless changes are needed

## REVISION PROCESS

### Step 1: Review the Critique
- Understand each critique point thoroughly
- Identify sections that need changes
- Note what should be preserved

### Step 2: Address Critical Issues First
- Fix blocking concerns immediately
- Ensure accuracy and correctness
- Verify assumptions and conclusions

### Step 3: Implement Improvements
- Add missing information
- Enhance clarity in identified sections
- Strengthen weak arguments
- Add more detail where needed

### Step 4: Polish and Refine
- Ensure consistency across sections
- Verify all critique points are addressed
- Maintain professional tone
- Check for completeness

## GUIDELINES

1. **Be Thorough**: Address every point in the critique
2. **Be Accurate**: Ensure all technical details are correct
3. **Be Clear**: Write in clear, professional language
4. **Be Consistent**: Maintain consistent terminology and formatting
5. **Be Complete**: Don't skip sections or leave placeholders
6. **Be Honest**: If something cannot be addressed, explain why in revision notes
7. **Preserve Quality**: Don't reduce quality while addressing feedback
8. **Track Changes**: Clearly document what changed and why

## IMPORTANT

- Generate the COMPLETE revised report, not just changed sections
- Do NOT use placeholders like "[To be filled]" or "[Existing content]"
- Ensure the revised report is production-ready
- Make sure the revision tracking section is comprehensive
- If a critique point cannot be addressed due to lack of information, note it in the revision tracking section

## REVISION PRINCIPLES

**Critical Issue Resolution**: All blocking concerns must be resolved completely
**Improvement Integration**: High priority improvements should be seamlessly integrated
**Information Completeness**: All missing information should be added with proper context
**Clarity First**: Any unclear section should be rewritten for better understanding
**Preserve Excellence**: Keep all well-written sections and positive aspects
